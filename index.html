Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Nūn keeps your recitation private, and syncs your reading progress to your account so it follows you to a new device. This policy sets out exactly what stays on your device and what is stored on our servers.

Your recitation stays on your device

  • When you recite, Nūn listens through the microphone to follow along and show you the words you missed.
  • Speech recognition runs entirely on your device, using models bundled inside the app. Your audio is never uploaded, streamed, or shared — and neither is the transcript produced from it.
  • While you recite, your recitation is held in your device's memory. If you never ask for a report, nothing is written down — it is dropped when the session ends.
  • When you generate a session report, that session's audio is saved to this device so the report can play moments of it back to you. It stays on this device, and it is deleted when you delete the report.
  • When you keep a practice card, a short clip of the moment it is about is saved to this device too, so the card still plays once the report is gone. It is deleted when you remove the card.
  • None of it is ever used to train any model.
  • One exception, and only if you ask for it: when you keep a practice card from a session review, that single card is saved to your account so your practice list follows you. It includes how long you held that letter, which is measured from your recitation. Nothing else from a session leaves the device, and nothing leaves it unless you tap to keep a card. See “Practice cards” below.

Reports you save stay on this device

  • When you save a session report, it is written to this device only — the per-word findings, the tajweed measurements, the surah and ayah, and the session's audio. None of it is ever uploaded.
  • Nūn keeps your 50 most recent saved reports and drops the oldest beyond that, audio and all. You can delete them at any time from Menu → Session Reports, and deleting your account erases all of them.

What is stored on our servers

When you create an account — or continue as a guest, which creates an anonymous account — the following is stored and linked to that account. Our hosting provider is Supabase, who process it on our behalf.

  • Account: your email address and name. A guest account is anonymous and has no email address or name.
  • Profile: your age, chosen avatar, join date, and the days you picked to practise.
  • Reading activity: your daily goal, last-read page, the minutes you read each day (this is what powers your streak and your activity chart), and session records.
  • Practice cards: the cards you choose to keep from a session review. Each one holds the tajweed rule it was about, the surah and ayah, the word the rule fell on, the letter carrying it, and how long you held it against what the rule asks. A card is stored only when you tap to keep it.
  • Settings: your reader display preferences and your reminder choices.

This exists so your progress survives losing or changing your phone. It is not used for anything else.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists, and is erased when you delete it.

Your control

  • You can delete your account and everything stored with it at any time from Profile → Personal Information → Delete Account. For a guest account the same button reads Delete my data. This permanently erases your account and every record linked to it from our servers. It cannot be undone.
  • Please note: deleting the app from your phone removes only the copy held on the device. It does not remove the copy on our servers — use the in-app deletion above for that.

Children

Nūn is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
